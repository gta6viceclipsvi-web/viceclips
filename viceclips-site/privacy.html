Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 8, 2026

This policy explains what the Vice Clips Publisher ("the app") does with data. The app is used only by the Vice Clips operator to post to the operator's own accounts.

Data the app uses

  • Sign-in tokens issued by YouTube, TikTok and Instagram after the operator signs in on the platform's own page. The app never sees or stores passwords.
  • Basic account details of the operator's own accounts: the display name or username, to show which account is connected, and, for TikTok, the privacy options the account allows.
  • The videos and captions the operator chooses to publish.

How it is used

Only to upload and publish the operator's chosen videos to the operator's own accounts and to show whether each upload worked.

Where it is stored

Tokens and a log of published posts are stored only on the operator's own computer. Nothing is sent to any server other than the official YouTube, TikTok and Meta (Instagram) APIs.

Sharing

The app does not sell, share or rent any data, does not track viewers, and uses no advertising or analytics.

Other people's data

The app does not collect data about viewers, followers or other users of the platforms.

Removing access

The operator can disconnect any account in the app, which deletes its token, and can revoke access at any time in the platform's own settings (TikTok: Settings and privacy, Security, Apps and services; Google: myaccount.google.com/permissions; Facebook: Settings, Business integrations).

Contact

Send a direct message to @GTA6ViceClipsVI on Instagram, TikTok or YouTube.
